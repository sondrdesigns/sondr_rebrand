`StickyNote` — a tilted yellow post-it for short asides and annotations.

```jsx
<StickyNote tilt={4}>ship it friday!</StickyNote>
<StickyNote color="#c9e5ff" size={180}>revisit copy</StickyNote>
```

Props: `tilt` (deg), `color`, `size` (px). Keep text short — it's a note, not a paragraph. Rendered with the mono face and a lift shadow.
