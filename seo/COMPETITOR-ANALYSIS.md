## Tier 1: Direct Local Competitors (Hawaii)

**Bucket A — Full-service digital marketing agencies** (larger, SEO/ads/social focus, web design is secondary)  
- Typically rank for "hawaii web design" with high-volume, generic content  
- Weak portfolio presentation; template-heavy work  
- Target: SMBs wanting bundled services

**Bucket B — Freelancers/solo designers**

**Sondr's gap to fill:** No Hawaii agency currently occupies the "premium boutique studio" position with high-quality case studies, hand-coded sites, and a distinct aesthetic identity. This is an open door.

## Tier 2: National Boutique Design Studios

These studios compete for the "bespoke web design" and "hand-coded" keyword space nationally. They have significantly higher domain authority but are not local Hawaii competitors.

### Studio Archetype: Premium Boutique

**Typical characteristics:**
- DA 30–55
- 50–200 pages indexed
- Strong case study library (10–30 detailed studies)
- Active blog (2–4 posts/month)
- Team pages with bios and headshots
- Service pages at `/services/web-design`, `/services/branding`, etc.
- Multiple industry vertical pages

**Sondr's national keyword opportunity:**
- "no-template web design" — very few agencies own this long-tail
- "hand-coded website" — emerging search interest
- "paper-first design process" — zero competition (own this phrase)
- "bespoke website hawaii" — geographic modifier reduces competition
