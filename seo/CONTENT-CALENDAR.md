## Content Format Guidelines

### Blog Post Structure (minimum 800 words)
1. Hook — a specific, surprising observation from real project work
2. Context — why this matters to a Hawaii small business
3. Body — 3–5 concrete points, each grounded in real examples
4. CTA — soft link to relevant service or "start a project"
5. Author bio — name, role, photo, LinkedIn

### Case Study Structure (minimum 1,000 words)
1. Client and industry
2. The brief and the challenge
3. Our approach (with process photos/sketches if possible)
4. Key design decisions and why
5. Outcome with specific metric (required)
6. Client quote (required)
7. Related services CTA
