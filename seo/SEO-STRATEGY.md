## E-E-A-T Strategy

Sondr's primary E-E-A-T challenge: as a new studio (est. 2024), it has limited digital footprint. The content plan is designed to build E-E-A-T signals fast.

**Experience signals:**
- Publish case studies with specific outcomes (KHM Tutoring already has a "30% more enquiries" metric — use it)
- Document the design process in blog posts with real project screenshots

**Expertise signals:**
- Team bio pages with individual LinkedIn profiles linked
- Author attribution on blog posts
- Person schema for each team member

**Authoritativeness signals:**
- Client testimonials on case study pages
- Featured in local Hawaii business directories and press
- Social proof: Instagram following and activity

**Trustworthiness signals:**
- Physical address displayed (Honolulu, HI)
- Studio hours displayed
- Phone number (add to schema and site)
- Privacy policy page
